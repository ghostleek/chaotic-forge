## Outcome

A bounded feasibility experiment determines whether camera-observed hand signs can control a playable jump quest with original platformer enemies, usable latency, and a clear fallback. The Naruto-sign/Mario-like references describe the desired experience; they do not imply licensed assets or an already implemented AR runtime.

Read [PRD](../../PRD.md), [PLAN](../../PLAN.md), [QUALITY](../../QUALITY.md), [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md), accepted runtime/input contracts, and [OpenAI capability assessment](../openai-capability-fit.md). Verify the actual gesture library, browser capabilities, and camera/AR requirements before choosing an implementation.

Allowed: `lib/party-forge/gesture/**`, `components/party-forge/gesture/**`, `app/party/gesture-lab/page.tsx`, `public/party-forge/gesture-lab/**`, `tests/party-gesture.test.mjs`, `tests/browser/party-gesture.spec.ts`, and `docs/party-gesture-feasibility.md`. Package/lockfile or hosting changes remain Lance-owned and require coordination. Do not retrofit camera controls into the accepted FPS demo.

First prove camera permission, one intentional sign recognized reliably, a second distinguishable sign, calibrated/debounced input, and a usable jump quest. Measure mistaken activations, missed gestures, latency, fatigue, lighting sensitivity, and supported devices. Use actual camera input for acceptance; synthetic landmarks can test classification logic but do not establish camera usability.

Define whether the prototype is camera overlay, gesture-controlled play, or genuinely world-anchored AR. Label it accurately. Provide keyboard controls, permission-denied recovery, clear camera-active state, stop/release behavior, and a consented processing path. Default to local processing and no camera recording/upload; any external video inference needs explicit scope and a real supported service.

Use original simple enemies/assets. A single authored test scene is enough for feasibility. Generating scenes on demand remains PC-09; hand recognition does not establish game generation. Keep all generated/downloaded asset and model provenance documented.
