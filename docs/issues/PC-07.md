**Status:** Local issue draft; no GitHub issue/PR created, verified, or assigned.

## Outcome

Three friends in separate browsers choose cards, play, see results, let the winner and loser each add a mechanic, play again, agree to end, and save or revisit their game. The integration consumes real room, runtime, and archive behavior.

Read [PRD](../../PRD.md), [PLAN](../../PLAN.md), [QUALITY](../../QUALITY.md), [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md), and accepted PC-03/04/05/06 interfaces.
