## Outcome

Three separate browsers share one competition: initial cards, matched 60-second trials, results, one winner addition and one loser addition, then another round or unanimous group end. The server enforces the rules even when clients submit duplicate, stale, or unauthorized commands.

Read [PRD](../../PRD.md), [PLAN](../../PLAN.md), [QUALITY](../../QUALITY.md), [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md), PC-01/02 contracts, and the state-continuity/data-availability findings in [prior review](../../archive/2026-09-13-mechanic-lab/mechanic-forge-pr-review.md).
